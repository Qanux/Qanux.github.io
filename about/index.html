About Me

My name is Junxi Qian. I am currently pursuing my Master's degree in Network and Information Security at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS). I completed my undergraduate studies in Computer Science and Technology at South China Normal University (SCNU), where I was the captain of the Sloth and participated in CTFs through union teams W&M and S1uM4i.

My research interests encompass software security, operating system security, and AI for security. Meanwhile, I am actively learning about post‑training techniques for large language models, such as fine‑tuning, reinforcement learning, and model alignment, with an eye toward integrating them into security research.
